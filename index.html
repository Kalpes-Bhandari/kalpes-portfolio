01 About

I was born in Goldhap, Jhapa, Nepal, to Bhutanese-Nepali parents. In 2009 my family came to the United States as refugees and settled in Des Moines, Iowa.

I studied Management Information Systems at Iowa State University, where I got hooked on data — not just the numbers, but how good data quietly powers every other part of a business. Today I live in the Harrisburg, Pennsylvania area, work full-time as a data engineer, and study for a master's in analytics (OMSA) online at Georgia Tech.

I've always wanted to be a polymath. Physics and math pull me in the most, but I like learning a little about everything.
